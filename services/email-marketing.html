What Is Email Marketing?

Email marketing is an effective digital marketing tactic businesses use to build client relationships, increase sales and raise brand awareness. Using leading Email Service Providers (ESPs), we send tailored, automated emails with your updates and promotions.

That covers a wide variety of messages — event invitations, customer surveys, promotional alerts, onboarding sequences and weekly newsletters. More than 4 billion people use email every day, and we help you reach the right ones at the right moment.
