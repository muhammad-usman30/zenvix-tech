Know the basics

What Is Website Development?

Website development is the process of designing, developing and maintaining websites. It brings together a number of disciplines — web hosting, web design, content development, search engine optimization and security implementation.

In simple terms, it's everything that goes into turning an idea into a fast, secure and easy-to-manage website that your customers can find on Google and enjoy using on any device.
